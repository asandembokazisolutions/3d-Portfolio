Student Performance Predictor
Regression model predicting math scores from academic & demographic factors
LIVE ＋
Overview

A linear regression pipeline predicting a student's math score from reading/writing scores and demographic factors. Preprocessing (one-hot encoding of categorical fields) is bundled into the same scikit-learn Pipeline as the model, so inference always mirrors training — no separate preprocessing step to fall out of sync.
